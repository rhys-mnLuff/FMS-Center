## Congressional App Challenge eligibility

Resolved as of 2026-09-01: Rhys is a U.S. citizen and Foreign Service dependent posted to Manila. CAC rules require U.S. residency at submission and competing in the district of residence or school attendance; the rules are silent on Americans posted abroad. Resolution path (in progress): identify the family's U.S. state of legal residence and its congressional district, confirm that district participates in CAC, and if ambiguous, get written confirmation from CAC directly.
