# Roadmap

Pulled directly from the project's live goal tracker. Status and target dates are real and current as of September 2026 — this file should be updated as goals move, not left to drift out of sync.

## North Star (target: September 2027)

**FMS is a deployed, recognized flood-warning system with documented real-world impact.**

Success criteria: at least one institutional partner has FMS installed; logged alert data from real conditions; at least one national or international recognition; a public writeup a stranger can read and understand.

Why it matters: this is the version of FMS that reads as extraordinary rather than as a school project — real units in real locations, real people relying on the alerts, outside recognition confirming it. Everything else below is a step toward this.

## Why "FMS survives a posting change" is on this list

Foreign Service postings end. If FMS only works while Rhys is in Manila holding it together, it's a project. If a Filipino partner can build, install, and service units from the documentation alone, it's infrastructure. That's also the honest version of the work — a flood system for Metro Manila should belong to the people who live with the flooding, not depend indefinitely on the person who happened to build the first one.

## What's deliberately not on this list yet

Multi-hop mesh routing, a finalized solar/battery spec, and a signed field deployment are all real next steps, but none of them have a committed target date in the tracker yet. They belong in this roadmap once they do — adding speculative dates here would undercut the point of keeping this file accurate.
